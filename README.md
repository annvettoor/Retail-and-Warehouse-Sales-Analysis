# Retail-and-Warehouse-Sales-Analysis
 To perform data visualization and generate meaningful business insights from warehouse and retail sales data.
 Tasks
1. Total Retail Sales Over Time
• Visualize total retail sales on a monthly, quarterly, and yearly basis.
• Identify seasonal patterns, growth trends, and decline periods.
2. Warehouse Sales Over Time
• Analyze warehouse sales trends over time.
• Compare warehouse movement with retail sales to understand supply-demand
alignment.
3. Item-Level Sales Trends
• Compare sales performance across different item categories.
• Identify high-performing and underperforming segments.
4. Sales Breakdown by Category
• Present a categorical breakdown of total sales.
• Highlight the contribution of each category to overall revenue.
5. Sales Breakdown by Department
• Analyze department-wise sales distribution.
• Identify departments contributing the highest and lowest sales.
6. Top N Selling Items
• Identify the Top N products based on:
o Units sold
o Sales value
• Interpret factors contributing to their performance.
7. Sales Distribution Analysis
• Create histograms to analyze the distribution of sales across products.
• Identify skewness, outliers, or concentration of sales among specific products.
8. Warehouse v/s Retail Comparison
• Develop side-by-side line or area charts comparing:
o Warehouse shipments
o Retail sales
• Interpret inventory flow patterns and supply-demand gaps.
9. Heatmap Analysis
• Create heatmaps showing:
o Warehouse outflows over time
o Retail inflows over time
• Identify peak periods and seasonal intensity.
10. Annual Growth Rate Analysis
• Calculate and visualize year-over-year growth rates for each category.
• Identify fast-growing and declining segments.
